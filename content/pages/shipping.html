Every listing shows exactly what shipping costs before you buy. Most parts go UPS Ground at a flat rate, heavy mechanical parts go by LTL freight, some parts ship free, and body parts and glass are pickup only. Here's the detail.

Flat-rate ground shipping

Most of what we sell goes UPS Ground to your door anywhere in the contiguous United States at a flat rate set by the part, from $9.99 to $44.99. Alternators, starters, modules, instrument clusters, radios, climate controls, mirrors, lights, A/C compressors, pumps, driveshafts and steering columns all ship this way. No commercial address and no forklift needed.

The exact rate is printed on every listing and charged at checkout — there is no surprise invoice afterward, and nothing to work out from a weight table. Some parts do ship free, and those say so on the listing too. We pack and hand off to the carrier within 1–2 business days, usually the next one, Monday through Friday. Transit is typically 2–5 business days depending on distance from Louisiana.

Freight items — flat rate, commercial address required

Some parts are too heavy for a parcel carrier and ship by LTL freight truck at a flat rate shown at checkout:

  • $299.99 — engines, transmissions and transaxles, axle assemblies and loaded beam axles, K-frames, radiator core supports, and vehicle batteries
  • $199.99 — transfer cases and differential carriers

Batteries ship by freight, not parcel. A hybrid or 48-volt battery is a regulated dangerous good: it moves on a truck, to a commercial address, and cannot go by air, by parcel carrier, or to a house. Call 985-748-7195 before ordering one so we can confirm the destination will take it.

Freight must deliver to a business or commercial address with a dock or forklift — not a house. Terminal pickup and shipping to your mechanic both work. This is a hard requirement, and the reasons plus your options are laid out on our freight page. If you order freight to a residential address we'll call you before anything ships.

Pickup only — body parts, glass, and a few oversized items

We do not ship body panels or glass. Doors, hoods, fenders, quarter panels, bumpers, tailgates, decklids, pickup boxes, running boards, rocker panels, header panels, grilles, cowl vent panels, centre pillars, and all automotive glass are available for pickup at the yard or free local delivery only. They arrive damaged too often to ship responsibly, and crating them properly costs more than the panel is worth.

Other oversized or restricted items. A few parts are pickup-only for reasons of their own: seats, fuel tanks (residual fuel and vapour, which parcel carriers will not accept), and leaf springs. Every listing states its shipping method before you add the part to the cart.

Free local delivery

Local to Amite? We deliver free on our own truck — Amite, Hammond, Independence, Kentwood, Roseland, Tickfaw, and the rest of Tangipahoa Parish plus the surrounding area. Mention it when you order or call and we'll set it up. Local delivery covers everything, including body parts and glass.

Pickup at the yard

Free pickup at 59174 Hwy 51, Amite, Louisiana 70422, Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Central. Call ahead with your Part ID and we'll have it pulled and waiting at the counter, so you can look it over before it goes in your truck.

Tracking

You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as the label is created. Freight shipments get a pro number and the carrier calls to schedule an appointment.

If something arrives damaged

For parcel shipments, keep the packaging and call us. For freight, inspect before you sign and note any damage on the delivery receipt — once you sign clean, the carrier's liability ends. Either way call 985-748-7195 the same day and we handle the carrier claim, not you. Your 90-day warranty covers the part regardless.
