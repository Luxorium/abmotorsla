Ten acres, decades of inventory, and a counter where a person picks up the phone. Here's what's behind the parts you see on this site.

The lot

We sit on ten acres along Highway 51 in Amite. Vehicles come in from wrecks, insurance sales, and folks who call us to haul something off. They get inventoried, drained, and set out by section so our pullers can find them fast.

The rack

Once a part comes off a vehicle it gets a stock number, an inspection, a cleanup, and a shelf. That stock number is what ties everything together — it's on the part's tag, on this website, on your invoice, and in our system. When you call and give us a stock number, we know exactly which shelf to walk to.

The camera

Every part gets photographed on our own racks before it's listed. That's why the pictures on this site look like a salvage yard instead of a catalog: because they are. The part in the photo is the part that ships to you. No stock images, no "representative photo."

The counter

We're open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central, at 59174 Hwy 51, Amite, Louisiana 70422. Walk-ins are welcome. If you call ahead with a stock number, we'll have your part pulled and waiting.

If you're local, we'll deliver it to your shop or your house for free. If you're not, it ships — usually free, usually the same or next business day.
