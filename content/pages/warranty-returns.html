Every part we sell is covered by a 90-day money-back warranty, starting the day it arrives. This page explains exactly how that works, in plain language.

What's covered

All used parts sold by A&B Motors carry a 90-day money-back warranty against the part being defective, not working, or not matching what was listed. That covers:

  • The part is dead on arrival or fails within 90 days of delivery
  • The part doesn't match its listing — wrong casting number, wrong options, wrong side
  • The part was damaged in shipping

What isn't covered

  • Labor. We warranty the part itself. We can't cover the cost of installing or removing it, so please have a mechanic verify fitment before installation.
  • Consequential damage. Damage to other components caused by installation or use of the part.
  • Parts modified or disassembled after delivery, or parts with the stock-number tag removed.
  • Normal wear items sold as-is and clearly described that way in the listing.

How to start a return

  1. Call the yard at 985-748-7195, Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Central, or email sales@abmotorsla.com.
  2. Have your stock number ready — it's on the part's tag, on your invoice, and on the product page.
  3. We'll confirm the return and tell you where to send it. Keep the original tag attached.
  4. Once we receive and inspect the part, your refund goes back to the original payment method, typically within 3–5 business days.

Return shipping

If the part was defective, mis-listed, or damaged in transit, we cover return shipping. If you ordered the wrong part or changed your mind, return shipping is on you — but the part price is still refunded in full.

Cores and core charges

Some parts are sold with a core charge, which is refunded when your old unit comes back to us in rebuildable condition. Core charges are listed separately on the product page when they apply.
