Almost every part we sell is covered by a 90-day money-back warranty, starting the day it arrives. The exception is a small number of normal wear items sold as-is — and a listing that is one says so in its own description. This page explains exactly how that works, in plain language.

What the condition grades mean

Every listing states a condition, assessed when the donor vehicle was catalogued. Where the yard has graded a part, the listing shows that grade; where it has not, the listing says Used, inspected — the part was checked and is sound, but it has not been given a letter. A missing grade is not a worse part, it is less information, and the photographs and the description are the rest of the story.

That is the first of two checks. Most parts stay on the donor vehicle until they are sold, so we inspect the part again when we pull it for your order — and if it does not match its listing at that point, we call you before we ship it rather than sending it anyway.

What's covered

Used parts sold by A&B Motors carry a 90-day money-back warranty against the part being defective, not working, or not matching what was listed — every part except a wear item the listing itself describes as sold as-is. That covers:

  • The part is dead on arrival or fails within 90 days of delivery
  • The part doesn't match its listing — wrong casting number, wrong options, wrong side
  • The part was damaged in shipping

What isn't covered

  • Labor. We warranty the part itself. We can't cover the cost of installing or removing it, so please have a mechanic verify fitment before installation.
  • Consequential damage. Damage to other components caused by installation or use of the part.
  • Parts modified or disassembled after delivery, or parts with the stock-number tag removed.
  • Normal wear items sold as-is. A listing sold this way says so in its own description — if the description does not say as-is, the 90-day warranty applies. Ask us before ordering if you are unsure; we will read the description back to you over the phone.

How to start a return

  1. Call the yard at 985-748-7195, Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Central, or email sales@abmotorsla.com.
  2. Have your Part ID ready — it's on the part's tag, on your invoice, and at the top of the product page.
  3. We'll confirm the return and tell you where to send it. Keep the original tag attached.
  4. Once we receive and inspect the part, your refund goes back to the original payment method, typically within 3–5 business days.

Return shipping — and the shipping you already paid

If the part was defective, mis-listed, or damaged in transit, we cover return shipping and refund what you paid to have it sent to you — parcel or freight, the whole charge. If you ordered the wrong part or changed your mind, return shipping is on you and the original shipping is not refunded, but the part price is refunded in full. Pickup orders have no shipping charge either way. The refund policy sets this out row by row.

Cores and core charges

No listing on this site carries a core charge. The price shown is the whole price of the part: nothing is held back pending the return of an old unit, and there is no core to send us. If that ever changes, the listing itself will state the charge, how long you have to return the core, who pays that shipping, and what condition it has to come back in — a core charge without those four things is not something a buyer can act on.
