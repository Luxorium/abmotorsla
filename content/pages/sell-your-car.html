We buy cars and trucks — running or not, any year, any condition. Tell us what you've got and we'll give you a cash price over the phone, then come pick it up ourselves.

What we buy

  • Wrecked — front end, rear end, rollover, doesn't matter
  • Flood and storm damage
  • Blown engine or bad transmission
  • High mileage vehicles that aren't worth fixing
  • Non-running vehicles that have been sitting
  • Cars, pickups, SUVs, and vans — foreign and domestic, 1990s to current

How it works

  1. Call us at 985-748-7195 or send the details through the contact form.
  2. Tell us the year, make, model, mileage, and what's wrong with it. Photos help but aren't required.
  3. We give you a cash offer, usually on that same call.
  4. If you take it, we schedule pickup. Towing is on us — we haul it with our own truck.
  5. You get paid at pickup, and we handle the paperwork.

What to have ready

  • The title, in your name and signed over at pickup. No title? Call us anyway — depending on the situation there may still be a legal path, and we'll tell you straight.
  • Photo ID matching the title.
  • Your license plate removed — it stays with you, not the vehicle.
  • Anything of yours out of the glove box, console, and trunk.

Why sell to a recycler

A vehicle that's not worth repairing is still worth real money in parts. We put the good components on our shelves for somebody who needs them, and the rest gets recycled as steel instead of sitting in a field. You get cash and an empty driveway; somebody down the road gets their truck running for half of what a new part costs.

Where we pick up

We cover Amite and all of Tangipahoa Parish, plus Hammond, Independence, Kentwood, Ponchatoula, and the surrounding parishes. Farther out? Call and ask — we go where the vehicle is worth the trip.
