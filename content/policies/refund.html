Used parts sold by A&B Motors are covered by a 90-day money-back warranty beginning on the date the part is delivered or picked up. The single exception is a normal wear item expressly sold as-is, which the listing's own description states; where a description does not say as-is, the warranty applies.

What is covered

We will refund or replace a part that is defective, fails within the 90-day period, does not match the item described in the listing, or arrives damaged in shipping.

What is not covered

  • Labor, installation, or removal costs
  • Damage to other components resulting from installation or use of the part
  • Parts that have been modified, disassembled, or had the A&B Motors stock-number tag removed
  • Wear items expressly sold as-is and described that way in the listing

How to request a refund

Contact us at 985-748-7195 or sales@abmotorsla.com within 90 days of delivery, with your order number and the part's Part ID. We will confirm the return and provide the return address. Keep the original yard tag attached to the part.

Refunds

Once the returned part is received and inspected, we issue the refund to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 3–5 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

Shipping costs on a return

Two separate charges can be involved: what you paid to have the part sent to you, and what it costs to send it back. They are treated differently depending on why the part is coming back.

Reason for the returnOriginal shipping you paidReturn shipping

This applies to freight the same way it applies to parcel: where the mistake is ours, the freight charge you paid comes back with the price of the part. Pickup orders have no shipping charge either way.

Core charges

No listing on this site carries a core charge. The price shown is the whole price of the part: nothing is held back pending the return of an old unit, and there is no core to send us. If that ever changes, the listing itself will state the charge, how long you have to return the core, who pays that shipping, and what condition it has to come back in — a core charge without those four things is not something a buyer can act on.
