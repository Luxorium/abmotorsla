Refund and Return Policy

All used parts sold by A&B Motors are covered by a 90-day money-back warranty beginning on the date the part is delivered or picked up.

What is covered

We will refund or replace a part that is defective, fails within the 90-day period, does not match the item described in the listing, or arrives damaged in shipping.

What is not covered

  • Labor, installation, or removal costs
  • Damage to other components resulting from installation or use of the part
  • Parts that have been modified, disassembled, or had the A&B Motors stock-number tag removed
  • Wear items expressly sold as-is and described that way in the listing

How to request a refund

Contact us at 985-748-7195 or sales@abmotorsla.com within 90 days of delivery, with your order number and the part's stock number. We will confirm the return and provide the return address. Keep the original stock-number tag attached to the part.

Refunds

Once the returned part is received and inspected, we issue the refund to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 3–5 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

Return shipping

A&B Motors pays return shipping when the part is defective, was described incorrectly, or was damaged in transit. When a part is returned because the wrong item was ordered or is no longer wanted, the customer pays return shipping; the purchase price is refunded in full.

Core charges

Where a core charge applies, it is shown separately on the product page and is refunded when the old unit is returned to us in rebuildable condition.
