Shipping Policy

Processing time

Orders are pulled and packed within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday. Orders placed on a weekend or holiday are processed the next business day.

Standard shipping

Most parts ship free within the contiguous United States. Typical transit time is 2–5 business days after the part leaves our yard in Amite, Louisiana, depending on destination and carrier.

Freight and oversize items

Large or heavy parts — complete engines, transmissions, doors, hoods, bumpers, tailgates, bed assemblies, and axles — ship by freight carrier and are quoted individually based on destination and whether a loading dock or forklift is available. Please contact us at 985-748-7195 before ordering an oversize item so we can quote freight accurately.

Local delivery

Free local delivery is available in Amite and the surrounding area, including Tangipahoa Parish. Contact us to arrange delivery.

Local pickup

Free pickup at 59174 Hwy 51, Amite, Louisiana 70422, Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Central. Please call ahead with your stock number.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when your shipping label is created.

Damage in transit

Inspect your part on arrival. If it arrives damaged, keep all packaging and contact us right away at 985-748-7195. We file the carrier claim and make it right under our 90-day money-back warranty.
