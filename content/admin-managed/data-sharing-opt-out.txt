Your Privacy Choices — replacement wording
==========================================

NOT PUBLISHED BY store_setup.py, AND DELIBERATELY NOT LISTED IN site.json.

Shopify owns /pages/data-sharing-opt-out. `pageUpdate` accepts a write, returns no
userErrors, and discards it — verified 2026-09-04 both with "Use automated settings"
on and off. It is edited only in admin:

    Settings > Customer privacy > Data sharing opt-out page > Regions and content

Paste the three paragraphs below in place of Shopify's default prose. Do not touch the
opt-out form, the Global Privacy Control paragraph's mechanism, or the scripts — only
the narrative text.

Why it needs changing (audit C-05): the default says personal information "may be shared
with third parties, including advertising partners", while the Privacy Policy says the
opposite. The Policy is the accurate one — verified 2026-09-04: no Google, Meta, TikTok
or Pinterest pixel loads on the storefront, only Shopify's own trekkie and
web-pixels-manager, and the sales channels are Online Store, Shop and Point of Sale with
no marketing channel.

STANDING RULE: if a marketing or ads app is ever installed, or Shopify Audiences is
enabled, this page and the Privacy Policy must be revisited the same day. With automated
settings off, Shopify no longer updates the state list either.

--- paragraph 1 ---
As described in our Privacy Policy, we collect personal information from your
interactions with us and this website, including through cookies and similar
technologies. We use it to take and fill your order, to answer your questions, and to
understand how this site is used.

--- paragraph 2 ---
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third-party
advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising. A&B Motors runs no
third-party advertising, retargeting, or social-media tracking pixels on this site.
Sharing personal information for targeted advertising based on your activity across
different websites may be considered a "sale", "sharing", or "targeted advertising"
under certain U.S. state privacy laws — we do not carry out those activities. If that
ever changes, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy before it does.

--- paragraph 3 ---
The form below is still available if you want your preference recorded for this browser,
and for your customer account if you enter your email. If you visit our website with the
Global Privacy Control opt-out preference signal enabled, then depending on where you
are we will treat that as a request to opt out of any activity that may be considered a
"sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or other uses that may be considered
targeted advertising, for the device and browser you used.

--- not legal advice ---
The audit asks for a counsel-reviewed disclosure. This wording makes the two pages agree
with each other and with the evidence; it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
