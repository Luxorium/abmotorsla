Blocking — no orders without these

Both are account and banking setup gated behind identity verification. There is no API for either.

Turn on paymentsyou

Settings → Payments → Complete account setup. Have ready: legal business name and EIN, business address, owner date of birth, and a bank routing and account number.

Decide first which entity is the merchant of record. Two exist at your address — A & B Motors, Inc. (corporation, 1968) and A & B Auto, LLC (2005). The legal name, EIN, and bank account on the application must all match the same one. A mismatch is the most common cause of frozen payouts during review.

Set the statement descriptor to ABMOTORS so buyers recognize the charge and don't file chargebacks. Then enable Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal — they're toggles.

Worth adding: a manual payment method named "Pay at the yard" for local pickup orders. Costs nothing and matches how you already do business.

Turn on taxyou

Settings → Taxes and duties → United States → collect sales tax for Louisiana, and enter your LA Department of Revenue account number. Leave Shopify Tax on; it handles state plus parish rates at the rooftop level.

Two things to know: Shopify does not remit for you — unlike eBay, which files as a marketplace facilitator, you are the retailer of record here. And you only owe tax where you have nexus, which today is Louisiana only.
